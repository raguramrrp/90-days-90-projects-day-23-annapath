# ANNAPATH — Project Overview

> **Tagline:** Good food deserves another destination.  
> **Initial Market:** India (Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad)  
> **Core Category:** Community Food Redistribution & Surplus Management Platform

---

## 1. Problem Statement
In India, an estimated **68 million tonnes of food is wasted annually**, while millions face food insecurity. Luxury hotels, wedding banquet halls, corporate cafeterias, and artisanal bakeries regularly produce surplus food of impeccable quality that must be cleared at the end of buffet shifts or daily baking cycles.

Currently, this surplus is either discarded into municipal landfills (generating potent methane emissions) or redistributed through fragmented, uncoordinated phone calls lacking hygiene verification, temperature tracking, or auditable delivery records.

## 2. Product Objectives
ANNAPATH bridges this gap through a multi-role, verified redistribution platform:
1. **Empower Food Providers:** Enable hotels, restaurants, and caterers to publish safe, surplus meals at prices they choose (₹0 Free or nominal amounts to recover packaging costs).
2. **Accessible Discovery:** Provide recipients and community kitchens with geo-sorted listings, interactive maps, and transparent dietary/allergen disclosures.
3. **Double Verification (OTP):** Prevent phantom handovers and maintain accountability via cryptographically hashed One-Time Passwords for direct pickups and volunteer deliveries.
4. **Volunteer Network:** Coordinate gig-style volunteer delivery runs without penalizing volunteers for declining tasks.
5. **Defensible Impact Metrics:** Audit actual meals handed over, strictly separating listed food from genuinely redistributed food.

---

## 3. Scope & MVP Boundaries

### In Scope (MVP Release)
- 5 Distinct Roles: Hotel Owner, Hotel Staff, Volunteer, Recipient, Platform Administrator.
- Verified Provider Outlet management with FSSAI license capture.
- Surplus listing creation with mandatory hygiene suitability confirmation.
- Dual pricing model: ₹0 Free meals vs. provider-controlled nominal pricing.
- Haversine distance-based discovery feed and Leaflet map exploration.
- Atomic inventory reservation locking with zero oversubscription risk.
- Counter pickup OTP verification.
- Volunteer delivery assignment, transit tracking, and recipient OTP verification.
- In-app notification center.
- Strict impact analytics (landfill kg saved and CO2e emissions prevented).
- Admin verification queue, complaint resolution, and audit logs.

### Out of Scope (Future Phases)
- Integrated online payment gateway (cash / pay-at-counter in MVP).
- Automated AI dispatch routing algorithms.
- WhatsApp Business API automated broadcasts.
- Multi-country currency conversions.
