## 2. Recipient Guide

### How to Discover Surplus Food
1. Click the **Discover Surplus** tab.
2. Toggle between **List View** and **Map View** to see partner locations.
3. Use category filters (Full Meals, Bakery, Curries, Snacks, Desserts) or search keywords.
4. Click **₹0 Free Only** to view complimentary community batches.

### How to Reserve Food
1. Click **Reserve Portions** on any card.
2. Select your desired portion count (up to the provider's max limit).
3. Choose **Direct Pickup** or **Volunteer Delivery**:
   - If volunteer delivery, enter your street address and gate notes.
4. Click **Confirm Reservation**.

### Collecting Your Food
1. Go to **My Reservations**.
2. Locate the reservation card displaying your **6-digit Collection OTP**.
3. Visit the hotel counter within the safe collection window and share your OTP with staff.
4. If you chose volunteer delivery, wait for the courier and give them your OTP upon doorstep handover.

## 3. Hotel Owner & Kitchen Staff Guide

### Publishing Surplus Food
1. Go to **Surplus Listings** and click **Publish Surplus Food**.
2. Fill in:
   - Batch Title and detailed description
   - Food Category (Meals, Bakery, etc.)
   - Portion size (e.g. 1 Plate ~500g)
   - Available portion count
   - Free (₹0) or Nominal price per portion
   - Safe collection deadline (e.g. 4 hours)
3. Check the mandatory box: **"I confirm that this food has been stored under hygienic conditions and is fit for human consumption in accordance with FSSAI regulations."**
4. Click **Publish Surplus Batch**.

### Verifying Pickups at Kitchen Counter
1. Open the **Counter OTP Desk** tab.
2. Select the awaiting recipient from the dropdown.
3. Ask the recipient for their 6-digit OTP code and type it into the input.
4. Click **Verify & Hand Over Package**.
5. The system confirms the cryptographic match and updates the portion to redistributed status.

## 4. Volunteer Guide

### Finding & Accepting Deliveries
1. Open the **Available Deliveries** tab.
2. Review the hotel pickup location, distance, recipient address, and portion quantity.
3. Click **Accept Delivery Mission**.

### Completing the Delivery Run
1. Step 1: Reach Hotel -> Click **I Have Reached Hotel**.
2. Step 2: Receive packages from staff -> Click **Confirm Package Handover**.
3. Step 3: Arrive at recipient's door -> Ask recipient for their **Delivery OTP**, type the 6 digits into the field, and click **Verify & Complete Drop**.
4. The mission completes and your impact statistics update!

## 5. Platform Administrator Guide

1. **Provider Verification:** Review business applications and check FSSAI license numbers. Click **Approve** or **Suspend**.
2. **Volunteer Moderation:** Verify volunteer documents and approve active statuses.
3. **Safety Complaints:** Review filed complaints regarding temperature, packaging, or counter delays. Add investigation notes and click **Close & Resolve Case**.
4. **Audit Trail:** Inspect the immutable event log showing timestamps, actor IDs, and handover actions.
